# SynthScan — The Open Content-Authenticity Manifesto

> **SynthScan** — we scan for synthetic content. Text. Images. Code. Anything AI-made.

One page that explains **who we are, what we refuse to do, and why open beats closed** for AI-content verification. This doubles as the project's README/intro that wins us our first users and contributors.

---

## Our Mission

**Pangram, GPTZero, and Originality are closed, paid, black-box services that profit from judging people's writing.**

We believe verifying whether content was written by a human or generated by AI should be:

- **Open** — the code must be auditable by anyone.
- **Free** — no one should be priced out of knowing the truth about a document.
- **Private** — your text should never have to leave your hands.
- **Fair** — a score is a signal to investigate, never a verdict to punish.

So we're building the first genuinely **open-source, self-hostable, community-updated** content-integrity toolkit. **It does what they do. What they do to us, we refuse to do to you.**

## How We Stay Current (no expensive, secret retraining)

Getting it right about new AI models is not a closed-team secret. Our way:

1. **Zero-shot detection by default.** We build on academically solid, open zero-shot detectors (e.g. Binoculars, Radar/RAID) that work on *any* LLM — no per-model retraining needed for most launches.
2. **Community sample pool.** When a new model ships, anyone can contribute sample outputs to our open dataset.
3. **Open, periodic refreshes.** When retraining *is* warranted, it happens in the open, collaboratively — never as a hidden, paid-only upgrade.

Pangram's team privately and automatically retrains. **We refresh openly, together, transparently** — and often at zero training cost.

## What "Self-Host" Means (plain English)

> Right now, using Pangram means pasting your text into **their** website, on **their** servers, paying per month.
>
> **Self-hosting** means you run the open-source software on **your own machine** — your laptop, a school's server, a $5/month VPS. The analysis happens locally. **Your text never leaves your hands.**

It's the difference between renting a washer at the laundromat (their building, their fee, they see your clothes) and owning a machine at home (your house, no fee, your data stays in your walls — and it keeps working even if the store closes).

**Who needs this:**
- Universities holding FERPA/GDPR-protected student work
- Law firms, government, and regulated industries that legally can't send documents to a third-party cloud
- Independent journalists and publishers protecting drafts
- Anyone who wants to know the truth without paying or being tracked

This is a market Pangram **structurally cannot serve** without abandoning its own business model.
